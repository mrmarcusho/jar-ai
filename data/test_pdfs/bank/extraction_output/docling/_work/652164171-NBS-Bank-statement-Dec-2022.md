AER

stands for Annual Equivalent Rate and shows what the interest rate would be if interest was paid

and compounded once a year.

Gross p.a. is the interest rate without tax deducted.

Our per year compounded

interest rate shows the rate of interest that you'll pay on your overdraft over a

year and takes account of the fact we charge interest each month on your balance and this will include any

interest which has already been added to your account previously.
